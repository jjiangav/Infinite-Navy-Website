Privacy Policy

Infinite Navy · Infinite Skies Studios · Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Infinite Navy game (the "Game") and this website collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean Infinite Skies Studios, based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. If you have any question, write to support@infinitenavy.com.

The short version

  • The Game makes an anonymous account for you when you first play, so your gold and purchases are kept safe. Adding an email is optional.
  • We collect what the Game needs to work: your account, gold, ships, Marketplace activity, and purchase records.
  • We do not show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, collect your location or contacts, or sell your information.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • You can delete your account at any time. Here's how.

1. Information we collect

Information created when you play

  • Account ID. When you first open the Game, it creates an anonymous account with a random ID, and stores a sign-in token on your device. This "guest" account holds your gold and purchases.
  • Game profile. Your gold balance; a record of gold earned and spent (for example missions won and when, parts and ships bought, refunds, Marketplace sales and purchases, gold packs); battles won; ships bought and sold; and the version of these terms you agreed to and when.
  • Captain name and ensign. The name and flag you choose for the Marketplace.

Information you give us

  • Email address, if you add one to sign in. We use it to send you one-time sign-in codes and messages about your account. It is never shown to other players.
  • Ships. Ship designs you save to your account (cloud ships), and ships you put on the Marketplace, with their name, description, price and a picture of the ship.
  • Bug reports, only if you send one: your description, the part of the game and how often it happens, and, if you leave them switched on, your email address, your device model and operating system version, the game version, the recent game log, and a screenshot of the game.
  • Messages you send to our support email.

Purchases

Gold packs are sold through Google Play, which processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Google sends us a purchase token and the product bought, which we check with Google before adding the gold to your account. We do not receive your name, card number or billing address.

On your device only

The ships in your Dock, your settings and your progress are saved on your phone. They stay there unless you save ships to your account or list them on the Marketplace.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos or files, microphone or camera, or advertising ID. The Game contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Game and your account: keeping your gold, ships and purchases, and letting you sign in on another device.
  • To run the Marketplace: showing listings, reviewing them before they go live, completing sales and telling you when something happens.
  • To keep the Game fair and secure: preventing cheating, fraud and abuse, and checking purchases are genuine.
  • To fix bugs and answer your questions.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as accounting and tax records, and to enforce our Terms of Service.

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the EU or UK), we rely on performing our contract with you (running the Game you asked for), our legitimate interests (security, preventing fraud, improving the Game), your consent (for optional bug-report details), and legal obligations.

3. What other players can see

On the Marketplace, other players can see your captain name and ensign, and the ships you list: their name, description, picture, design and price. When you buy a ship, its seller is told your captain name. Your email address and account ID are never shown to other players.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with:

  • Supabase, which hosts the Game's servers: the database, sign-in, file storage, and the emails with your sign-in codes.
  • Google, which distributes the Game and processes purchases through Google Play, and which we ask to confirm purchases.
  • Legal and safety. When the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our players, ourselves or others.
  • A new owner, if the Game or our business is sold or merged, who would have to respect this policy.

These providers may store and process information in Canada, the United States or other countries. Wherever it is processed, we require it to be protected as this policy describes.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account information is kept for as long as you have an account.
  • Guest accounts (with no email added) are deleted automatically once no gold has been earned or spent on them for 180 days, or for a year if a gold pack was bought on them. Uninstalling the Game removes a guest's sign-in from the phone, so such an account can't be used again.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, gold, gold history, notifications, cloud ships, sign-in and email, and any Marketplace listings that haven't sold.
  • Ships you have already sold stay with the players who bought them, with your captain name replaced by "Former captain".
  • Purchase records are kept, no longer linked to you, as long as needed for refunds, accounting and tax law.
  • Bug reports are kept for as long as they help us fix problems, and no more than two years.
  • Backups may hold deleted information for a short time before it is overwritten.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, have it deleted, take a copy with you, or object to or limit how we use it. You can:

  • Delete your account in the Game or by email. See Delete your account.
  • Change your captain name in the Game.
  • Make any other request by emailing support@infinitenavy.com. We may need to confirm the request comes from the account's owner, and we'll answer within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, or the data protection authority where you live.

7. Children

The Game is not made for children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to play. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Information travels between the Game and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to our servers is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise that information will never be lost or misused. We will tell you and the authorities about a breach where the law requires it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the date at the top, and if a change matters, we'll ask you to agree to it again in the Game.
