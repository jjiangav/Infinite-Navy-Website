Privacy Policy

Infinite Navy · Infinite Skies Studios · Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Infinite Navy game (the "Game") and this website collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean the developer of Infinite Navy, operating as Infinite Skies Studios, based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.

By using the Game or this website, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where the law requires your consent to any collection, use or disclosure described here, we ask for it separately (for example in the Game), and you can withdraw it as described in section 6.

The short version

This summary is a convenience. If it differs from the sections below, the sections below apply.

  • The Game makes an anonymous guest account for you when you first play, so your gold and purchases are kept. Adding an email is optional.
  • We collect what the Game needs to work: your account, gold, ships, Marketplace activity, and purchase records.
  • As of the effective date above, the Game does not show ads, does not include third-party analytics or tracking tools, does not collect your precise location or contacts, and we do not sell your personal information. If any of this changes, we will update this policy first.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your card details.
  • You can delete your account at any time: Delete your account.

1. Information we collect

Information created when you play

  • Account ID. When you first open the Game, it creates an anonymous guest account with a random ID and stores a sign-in token on your device. This guest account holds your gold and purchases.
  • Game profile. Your gold balance; a record of gold earned and spent (for example missions won and when, parts and ships bought, refunds, Marketplace sales and purchases, gold packs); battles won; ships bought and sold; and the version of our Terms you agreed to and when.
  • Captain name and ensign. The name and flag you choose for the Marketplace.

Information you give us

  • Email address, if you add one to sign in. We use it to send one-time sign-in codes and other messages about your account and the Game's service (for example security notices and changes to our terms). We do not use it for marketing unless you separately agree. It is never shown to other players.
  • Ships and Marketplace content. Ship designs you save to your account (cloud ships), and ships you list on the Marketplace, with their name, description, price and a picture.
  • Bug reports, only if you send one: your description, the part of the game and how often it happens, and, if you leave them switched on, your email address, device model and operating system version, the game version, the recent game log, and a screenshot of the game. Please do not include personal information about yourself or others in a bug report. You choose what to send.
  • Messages you send to our support email, and our replies.

Information collected automatically by our systems

  • Technical and security data. Like most online services, our servers and our service providers' systems may record your IP address, request times, app and device identifiers used for sign-in, and error and security logs. We use these to run and secure the Game and website, and keep them for up to 90 days unless needed longer to investigate abuse or a security incident.
  • Website. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log your IP address and the pages requested. Its fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads. This website does not use cookies, advertising or analytics.

Purchases

Gold packs are sold through Google Play, which processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Google sends us a purchase token and the product bought, which we check with Google before adding the gold to your account. We do not receive your name, card number or billing address from Google.

Information Google collects itself

The Game is distributed through Google Play and runs on Android. Google and your device manufacturer may collect information about your device and use of the Game under their own policies (for example crash and performance data through Google Play). We do not control that collection and this policy does not cover it.

On your device only

The ships in your Dock, your settings and your progress are saved on your phone. They stay there unless you save ships to your account or list them on the Marketplace.

What we do not collect

The Game does not request your precise location, contacts, photos or files, microphone, camera or advertising ID. Information that you choose to include in your own content (for example in a ship name or description, or in a bug report screenshot) is your responsibility.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Game and your account: keeping your gold, ships and purchases, and letting you sign in on another device.
  • To run the Marketplace: showing listings, reviewing them before they go live, completing sales and telling you when something happens.
  • To keep the Game fair and secure: preventing cheating, fraud and abuse, and checking purchases are genuine.
  • To fix bugs, improve the Game, and answer your questions.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as accounting and tax records, to respond to lawful requests, to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, and to enforce our Terms of Service.

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the EU or UK), we rely on performing our contract with you (running the Game you asked for), our legitimate interests (security, preventing fraud, enforcing our terms, improving the Game), your consent (for optional bug-report details), and legal obligations.

3. What other players can see

On the Marketplace, other players can see your captain name and ensign, and the ships you list: their name, description, picture, design and price. When you buy a ship, its seller is told your captain name. Your email address and account ID are not shown to other players.

Anything you put in a captain name, ship name, description or picture is public to other players and may be copied, screenshotted or shared by them outside our control. Do not include personal information in them. We may remove or edit content, but we cannot guarantee that every copy will be removed.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Supabase, which hosts the Game's servers: the database, sign-in, file storage, and the emails with your sign-in codes. The Game's servers are currently located in the United States (Ohio). We may move them to another region or country, including Canada, the European Union or elsewhere in the United States, and will update this policy when we do.
  • Google, which distributes the Game and processes purchases through Google Play, and which we ask to confirm purchases.
  • Our service providers and advisers (for example hosting, email, security, accounting, legal and insurance providers), only as needed to give us their services and subject to confidentiality.
  • Legal and safety. Where we believe disclosure is required or permitted by law (for example a court order, a lawful request from a government authority, or a legal claim), or is reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, abuse or harm, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our players, ourselves or others.
  • A new owner, if the Game or our business is sold, merged or reorganised, who would have to respect this policy for information already collected.

We and our providers may store and process your information in Canada, the United States or any other country where we or they operate, now or in the future, where it may be accessible to courts and authorities under local law. Where required, we use contractual safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and require our providers to protect information at a level consistent with this policy.

5. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, and in any event for the periods below, which are maximums unless the law or a legal claim requires longer.

  • Account information is kept for as long as you have an account.
  • Guest accounts (with no email added) are deleted automatically once no gold has been earned or spent on them for 180 days, or for one year if a gold pack was bought on them. Uninstalling the Game removes a guest's sign-in from the phone, so that account can no longer be used.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, gold, gold history, notifications, cloud ships, sign-in and email, and any Marketplace listings that have not sold.
  • Ships already sold stay with the players who bought them, with your captain name replaced by "Former captain".
  • Purchase and accounting records are kept for as long as needed for refunds, chargebacks, accounting, tax and audit purposes (generally six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as Canadian tax law requires). We unlink them from your profile where we can.
  • Bug reports are kept for as long as they help us fix problems and no more than two years.
  • Security and technical logs are kept as described in section 1.
  • Backups may hold deleted information for up to 30 days before being overwritten.
  • We may keep information for longer where we reasonably need it to comply with the law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or enforce our terms, including a record that an account was removed for breaking our rules.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, have it deleted, take a copy with you, withdraw consent, or object to or limit how we use it. To make a request:

  • Delete your account in the Game or by email. See Delete your account.
  • Change your captain name in the Game.
  • Email support@infinitenavy.com with the subject "Privacy request". We may need to confirm the request comes from the account's owner. We aim to answer within 30 days, or tell you if we need more time as the law allows.

Your rights are subject to limits in the law. We may refuse or limit a request where the law allows, for example where it would reveal another person's information, affect legal privilege, interfere with an investigation or legal claim, or is manifestly unfounded or excessive. Withdrawing consent or deleting information may mean the Game or some features can no longer work for you.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first so we can try to fix it. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, or the data protection authority where you live.

7. Children

The Game is not made for children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to play. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we may delete it and its information, and we will not be responsible for the loss of its Virtual Items. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it. If you are under the age of majority where you live, a parent or guardian is responsible for your use of the Game, including purchases.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the Game and our servers and restricted access to our servers. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be lost, accessed or misused. You are responsible for keeping your device and email account secure. We will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires it.

9. Links and third parties

The Game or website may link to third-party sites or services (for example Google Play). We do not control and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date at the top. For changes that materially affect how we use your personal information, we will give notice in the Game or by email and, where the law requires, ask for your agreement again. Where the law allows, continuing to use the Game after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.

11. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Province of British Columbia and the federal laws of Canada that apply there, without limiting any mandatory privacy rights you have where you live.
