The shipyard

Every hull starts as a single block

Lay the keel, stack the superstructure, place the funnels. Blocks snap to a grid, so a destroyer takes minutes and a 2,000-block carrier takes as long as you like.

  • Body, Engine, Weapon, Aircraft and Decoration parts in one catalog.
  • Weight and balance matter. Top-heavy ships roll; underpowered ones crawl.
  • Spin the model on every part's info page before you spend the gold.

Marketplace

Sell your best design for gold

Built something other captains will want? List it. Every ship sells once, to one buyer, at the price you set.

  1. Pick a ship from your DockIt leaves your Dock while it's listed.
  2. Set your priceAt least what its parts are worth, up to a cap.
  3. We review itEvery listing is checked before it goes live.
  4. It sells, you get paidThe buyer gets the ship and the gold lands in your balance.
