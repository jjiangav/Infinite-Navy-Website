Delete your Infinite Navy account

Infinite Navy · by Infinite Skies Studios

You can delete your Infinite Navy account and its data at any time, in the Game or by email.

Deleting is permanent. Your gold (including gold you bought), your captain and your cloud ships can't be restored afterwards, even by us, and gold isn't refunded.

Option 1: in the Game

  1. Open Infinite Navy and go to the Marketplace.
  2. Open your Captain profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and follow the steps to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email

If you can't use the Game, or never added an email to your account, email support@infinitenavy.com with the subject Delete my account. Include:

  • the email address you sign in with, if you have one (send your request from that address), and your captain name; or
  • if you play as a guest without an email, a gold pack order number from your Google Play receipt, or a description of your account, so we can find it.

We may ask you to confirm the request is yours. We will delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Guest accounts are deleted automatically

If you play without adding an email, you have a guest account. Uninstalling the Game removes its sign-in from your phone, and we delete guest accounts automatically once no gold has been earned or spent on them for 180 days (a year if you bought a gold pack). You can also ask us to delete one sooner, as above.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and email address
  • Your captain name, ensign and game profile: gold balance, gold history, battles won, ships bought and sold
  • Your cloud ships
  • Your Marketplace listings that haven't sold, and the ships in them
  • Your notifications

What is kept

  • Ships you already sold stay with the players who bought them. Your captain name on them is replaced by "Former captain".
  • Purchase records (which gold pack was bought, and when) are kept, no longer linked to you, as required for refunds, accounting and tax.
  • Bug reports you sent are kept, no longer linked to your account, for up to two years.
  • Backups may hold your data for a short time before it is overwritten.

Ships saved only on your phone stay there until you uninstall the Game. The Game can delete them too when you delete your account.

For more, see our Privacy Policy.
